Yogananda

Engineer by day, photographer the rest of the time. Left brain, right brain, both fully committed.

Why I do this

Photography started as a way to hold onto things. People change, days end, kids get bigger overnight — and I hated how fast all of it moved. So I picked up a camera.

Now I get to freeze the in-between moments, the ones that aren't posed but somehow say the most. When you look back at these photos in ten years, I want you to feel that day again, not just see it.

How I work

Honestly, the best photos happen when no one's trying. So that's what I build my whole process around. Before the shoot, I learn what you care about. During it, I stay light on my feet and let the day unfold — I'm watching for the in-between moments more than the posed ones.

After, I sit with every image and edit by hand until it feels right. You stay in the moment. I'll make sure none of it gets lost.

A few things about me

The engineering side makes me obsessive about details, so every frame in your gallery gets that care. Michigan-based but happy to travel for the right shoot. And you won't catch me saying "say cheese" — I'd rather catch you mid-laugh than mid-pose.
