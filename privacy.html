Privacy

Your details,
kept simple

The short version: this site doesn't track you, doesn't set cookies, and only ever gets what you choose to send me.

What this site collects

Nothing on its own. There are no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts, and nothing is loaded from other companies — even the fonts are served from this site. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, sees the technical details of a visit such as your IP address in order to deliver the page; GitHub's privacy statement explains how it treats them.

When you get in touch

The enquiry form doesn't store anything on this site. When you send it, the details are passed to FormSubmit, a service that forwards the message to my inbox and can see what it forwards. What you send — your name, email, phone, and the date and details of your shoot — comes to my inbox and is used only to reply and plan your photographs. I don't sell it, share it, or add you to a mailing list.

Photographs of you

If you're in a photograph on this site and would like it taken down, email me and I'll remove it promptly — from the site's history too, not just the page.

Deleting your details

Ask, and I'll delete our email conversation and anything else I hold about you, unless I still need it for a booking we have together.
