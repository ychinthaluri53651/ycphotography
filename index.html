Hey, I'm Yogananda

Photography started as a way to hold onto things. People change, days end, kids get bigger overnight — and I hated how fast all of it moved. So I picked up a camera.

Now I get to freeze the in-between moments: the ones nobody posed for, that somehow say the most.

Engineer by day, photographer the rest of the time. The engineering half just makes me obsessive about details — every frame in your gallery gets that care.

— Yogananda

How I work

The best photos happen when nobody's trying

Before the shoot, I learn what you actually care about. During it, I stay light on my feet and let the day unfold. After, I sit with every image and edit by hand until it feels right.

You stay in the moment. I'll make sure none of it gets lost. And you won't catch me saying "say cheese" — I'd rather catch you mid-laugh than mid-pose.
