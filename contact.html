Good to know

Questions I get asked

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings, as early as you can — peak season dates go 6 to 12 months out. Portraits, family sessions and events are usually much easier to fit in, sometimes within a couple of weeks.

Do you travel?

Yes. Michigan is home base, but I'll travel anywhere for the right shoot. Tell me where you are and I'll work out what travel would look like.

How many photos do I get, and when?

It depends on how long we're shooting for — a full wedding day runs into the hundreds. Everything arrives hand-edited in a private online gallery you can download, share and print from.

We're awkward in front of a camera. Help?

Almost everybody says this, and almost nobody ends up looking awkward. I don't do stiff posing — I give you something to do, then get out of the way and photograph what actually happens.

What does it cost?

Every shoot is different, so packages are put together around what you actually need rather than a fixed menu. Send me the details and I'll come back with options.
